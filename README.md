## I turn operational challenges into useful systems

At Western Digital, I help improve operational efficiency through digital transformation—moving from stakeholder discovery and requirements to workflow applications, AI/ML integration, testing, and implementation.

My work focuses on streamlining Head/Media qualification workflows and building digital tools that help teams work with greater agility, clarity, and confidence.

## What I do at WDC

- **Discover:** Conduct stakeholder interviews, understand requirements, and translate business needs into clear solution objectives.
- **Design:** Help design digital solutions that simplify processes and improve operational flow.
- **Build:** Develop, test, and support workflow applications for process automation.
- **Apply intelligence:** Integrate AI/ML models for predictive analysis and better decision-making within workflows.
- **Deliver:** Collaborate with IT and engineering teams on prototypes, system integration, user acceptance testing, and implementation.
- **Enable adoption:** Maintain project records, document findings, prepare user guidance when needed, and present recommendations to mentors and stakeholders.
